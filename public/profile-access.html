Common questions
Is this a sales pitch or a paid listing?

No. There is no cost to appear in the directory, and editing your profile does not enroll you in anything.

Where did this information come from?

From your public website and, where relevant, public sources like your GitHub organization. Nothing here was submitted by us to a third party or made up — and now you can correct anything that's off.

Will my changes go live right away?

Yes. Edits you save publish immediately to your public profile — there's no waiting on a reviewer for this. Removal requests are the one exception; those are still confirmed by a person first.

Can I come back and edit again later?

Yes. After your first save, this same link keeps working — bookmark it to update your listing anytime.
