Who is Rocket Engineers?

Some context, since this may be the first time you've heard from us.

What we are

Rocket Engineers is a directory that helps companies find vetted cloud, DevOps, and platform engineering partners across Switzerland, Germany, and the wider DACH region.

Why you're listed

Our team builds an initial profile from information already public on your company website, GitHub, and similar sources. Nothing here was submitted by us to a third party or made up.

What happens next

Claim your profile to correct details and manage it going forward, or request removal if you'd rather not be listed. A Rocket Engineers reviewer handles every request by hand — nothing changes automatically.

Common questions

Answers for companies hearing from Rocket Engineers for the first time.

Is this a sales pitch or a paid listing?

No. There is no cost to appear in the directory, and claiming your profile does not enroll you in anything. It gives you edit access to your own listing.

Where did this information come from?

From your public website and, where relevant, public sources like your GitHub organization. We do not use private data or make claims that aren't backed by that evidence.

What does claiming actually give me?

The ability to correct or expand your profile, add success stories and events, manage outreach contacts, and be considered for introductions from companies searching the directory.

What if I just want to be removed?

Submit a removal request above. A reviewer confirms the request came from the company before anything is taken down — this also protects you from someone else requesting removal on your behalf.

Will anything change publicly without me approving it?

No. Every claim, edit, and removal request is reviewed by a person before it affects the public profile.
