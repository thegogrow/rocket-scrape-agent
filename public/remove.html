What we are

Rocket Engineers is a directory that helps companies find vetted cloud, DevOps, and platform engineering partners across Switzerland, Germany, and the wider DACH region.

Why you're listed

Our team builds an initial profile from information already public on your company website, GitHub, and similar sources. Nothing here was submitted by us to a third party or made up.

What happens after a request

A Rocket Engineers reviewer confirms the request came from the company before anything is taken down. Nothing changes publicly without a person approving it first.
